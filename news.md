## 🌍 October 2026 – Research Stay at UMCG, Groningen

<p style="text-align: justify;">
<b>Svenja Seuffert</b> (PhD candidate) received a <b>research stay grant from the RTG of CRC/TRR 393</b> for a research visit at the <b>University Medical Center Groningen (UMCG), Netherlands</b>, in October 2026.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
During her stay, she is working on automated speech analysis in psychiatric disorders, with a focus on acoustic features and machine learning approaches. The visit also provides an opportunity to exchange methods and strengthen the collaboration between the research groups in Marburg and Groningen.
</p>

<br>

<!-- ===================== DISCOURSE – MARCH 2026 ===================== -->

## 🏆 March 2026 – IUSS Travel Grant for DISCOURSE in Psychosis (Pavia, Italy)

<p style="text-align: justify;">
<b>Svenja Seuffert</b> (PhD candidate) received an <b>International Travel Grant from the University School for Advanced Studies IUSS Pavia</b> to attend the <b>4th DISCOURSE in Psychosis Consortium Satellite Meeting</b>, held on March 30–31, 2026, in Pavia, Italy.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
The meeting, titled <i>“Language and Thought in the Brain and in Mental Health,”</i> brought together international researchers from psychiatry, neuroscience, psychology, and linguistics to discuss advances in understanding thought and language disturbances in psychosis.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
The travel grant was supported by the European Union's <b>Educating Future Citizens (EFC)</b> initiative, which promotes international mobility and interdisciplinary training for early-career researchers.
</p>

## 🏆 October 2025 – Poster Award for Dr. Alina Nostadt  

<p style="text-align: justify;">
<b>Dr. Alina Nostadt</b> was awarded a <b>Poster Prize at the 2025 CRC/TRR 393 Retreat</b> in Dresden for her outstanding poster:<br>
<i>“Transdiagnostic Identification of Cognitive Subtypes and White Matter Correlates.”</i>
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
The retreat brought together early-career researchers from all CRC/TRR393 sites and the International Research Training Group 2773, featuring nearly 70 posters across several scientific sessions. Posters were evaluated on scientific excellence, clarity of presentation, and design.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
<b>Congratulations to Alina on this well-deserved recognition!</b>
</p>

## 🏆 May 2025 – Awards at the DGBP Conference  

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Several members of the <b>NeuroSTA group</b> at the Department of Psychiatry and Psychotherapy, Philipps-University Marburg, were recognized with awards at this year’s annual meeting of the German Society for Biological Psychiatry (DGBP) in Berlin, Germany.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
<b>Dr. Frederike Stein</b>, Principal Investigator of <b>NeuroSTA</b>, received the <b>DGBP Research Award for Early Career Scientists</b> for her outstanding contributions to biological psychiatry.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Two NeuroSTA medical doctoral researchers were also honored for their poster presentations:
</p>

<ul>
  <li style="text-align: justify;">
    <b>Lea Kercher</b> received the <b>1st Poster Prize</b> for her work on the transdiagnostic analysis of delusional ideation.
  </li>
  <li style="text-align: justify;">
    <b>Lisa Koob</b> received the <b>3rd Poster Prize</b> for her research on sleep and depression.
  </li>
</ul>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
In addition to their poster awards, <b>both researchers received DGBP travel scholarships</b> to support their conference participation.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
The three-day conference featured a wide range of scientific talks, discussions, and networking opportunities for researchers in biological psychiatry. These awards highlight not only the quality of the presented work but also the strong commitment to supporting early-career researchers within the NeuroSTA group and at the Department of Psychiatry and Psychotherapy in Marburg.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
<b>Warm congratulations to all awardees, and sincere thanks to the DGBP for supporting early career researchers.</b>
</p>
